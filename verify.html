Verify a rip.

Every pack's secret seed is locked with a public SHA-256 hash before the first rip. Once the pack tears open the seed is revealed. Paste it here with the rip's client seed, its number and the odds at that moment, and your browser replays the roll. Nothing leaves this page.

Roll
The exact rule

roll = first 13 hex characters of HMAC-SHA256(key: server seed, message: "client seed:rip number"), read as a number, mod N. A roll of 0 tears the pack open. N starts at the slab's value divided by the rip cost and halves after every 100 rips without a tear.
