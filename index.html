01 — how it works

Fees stock it. You rip it.

One sealed pack on the table with one real graded slab inside. Everyone rips the same pack, and the rip that tears it open takes the slab home.

01

Fees stock the pack

Creator fees from every $RIPS trade buy a real graded slab and seal it in the next pack. Each slab is a Collector Crypt token backed by the physical card in their vault.

02

Rip it

Each rip burns a fixed $2 of $RIPS, priced the moment you click. Your rip rolls a number from 0 up to the odds. Roll a 0 and the pack tears open.

03

The slab goes to the wallet that tore the pack. Redeem the real card through Collector Crypt and have it shipped, or sell it back.

03 — questions

Before you rip.

What is RIPS?
A pack you rip open on chain. Creator fees buy a real graded slab and seal it in a pack, everyone rips the same pack, every rip burns $RIPS, and the rip that tears it open wins the slab.
Is the slab real?
Yes. Every slab is a Collector Crypt token backed by a physical graded card in their vault. You can redeem the card and have it shipped, or sell it back through Collector Crypt.
What does a rip cost?
A fixed $2 of $RIPS, priced when you click and burned on the spot. Every token spent goes to the Solana incinerator, where nobody can touch it again.
How do the odds work?
A pack starts at 1 in (slab value ÷ $2). Every 100 rips without a tear, the odds double in your favour, so every pack opens eventually.
Can I check it's fair?
Yes. Before the first rip the pack's secret seed is locked with a public SHA-256 hash. Each roll is HMAC-SHA256 of that seed with your own seed and a counter. When the pack opens the seed is revealed, and the verify page replays every roll in your browser.
When can I rip?
When $RIPS launches and the first pack is stocked. The contract address goes up here and on X at the same time.
Is RIPS connected to Pokémon?
No. RIPS is independent and not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Collector Crypt, Magic Eden or any grading company.
